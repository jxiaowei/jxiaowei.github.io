Privacy policy

Beyond the Headline uploader is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own YouTube channels.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to edit their titles, descriptions and thumbnails. The only data it stores is the sign-in token for those channels, kept privately on the owner's own computer. It does not collect, sell or share data about anyone, and it does not access other people's accounts.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy.
